How it works

Three steps at the machine

  1. 1

    Scan your QR

    Open this website on your phone, sign in, and hold your QR code up to the machine's reader.

  2. 2

    Drop your bottle

    Plastic in the green slot, glass in the blue one. The machine checks each bottle and crushes it so it can't count twice.

  3. 3

    Redeem your points

    Points arrive instantly. Pick a reward below and show your code at the partner business.

    FAQ

    Questions

    Do I need to download an app?

    No. Everything happens on this website: your QR code, your points and your rewards.

    Do I get cash for my bottles?

    No. REVA works only with points. Partner businesses fund the rewards because REVA brings them new customers.

    Do I have to wash my bottles?

    Empty them. A quick rinse is enough; they don't need to be spotless.

    What happens to the bottles?

    The machine sorts plastic and glass. Our team empties it and sells the material to local recyclers.

    What if a machine is full?

    Check the fill level in the Machines section before you go. A machine that's almost full is emptied within 24 hours.
